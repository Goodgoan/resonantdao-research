- Self-described "working specification". Each real action gets a sparse profile across 22 dimensions (Major Arcana as design vocabulary). Software proposes profiles and applies approved rules; humans govern definitions, evidence standards, calibration, review, appeals.
- Scoring: multidimensional profile per action; weights need not sum to one; verified delta ~ match x verified outcome (net of harm) x verification confidence x calibration. 22 non-transferable balances per member; unmeasured = null, never decays to zero. Pay on outcome, not activity (hours, attendance, frequency, pause length do not pay).
- Review tiers: Tier 0 automatic verification of deterministic events; Tier 1 machine proposals with audits; Tier 2 two independent reviewers for ambiguous/high-impact; Tier 3 rotating appeal panels. Reviewers record reasons, conflicts, appeal routes.
- Appeals without retaliation; unresolved stays pending. Calibration is a governed versioned parameter; Judgement (C_20) cannot finalise a cycle without current Justice (C_11) calibration. Every decision leaves an explanation record reproducible by a third party.
- Anti-farming list: metric capture, frequency farming, Sybil entry, manufactured care/teaching, conflict farming, closure/reflection theater, false accusations, reviewer capture, AI leverage capture. Human and AI portions attributed separately; voting counts only the human portion.
- Attribution: actions declare what informed them; downstream outcomes can pay back along provenance chains with governed decay, settled per cycle; provenance/attribution gaming flagged as risks.
- Tokens: C_0..C_21 non-transferable balances, on-chain representation undecided; $RCT derived approximate value, formula pending calibration; $RES separate transferable reward/utility token paid only when a class-specific outcome rule fires; holding/transferring $RES earns no contribution.
- Governance weight: proposals declare salient dimensions; weight from verified balances in those dimensions, capped; capital-derived contribution never enters voting weight; $RCT sets eligibility thresholds but never scales votes.
