The words we use

## One line each, before you read anything else

 AI Artisan
 A person who builds with AI as a craft — defined by vision, not code. Artists, writers, philosophers, scientists are explicitly included.
 Augmentatism
 The community’s shared philosophy and manifesto: take the third way between surrendering your mind to corporate AI and rejecting it — build sovereign AI worlds together.
 ResonantOS
 The community’s open-source product: a browser-first operating layer for working with AI in resonance. resonantos.com
 ResonantDAO
 The membership and governance layer around it all — this site. Contribution is recognized, not only capital.
 Resonant Academy
 The community’s learning space: peer-led sessions, every Tuesday on Stage.
 Discord
 Home base. The calls, the channels per project, the people. Join it here.
