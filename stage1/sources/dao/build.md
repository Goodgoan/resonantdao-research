 The community’s mission is to turn passive into active,
 consumers into producers. This page is for everyone standing at that
 edge: carrying a project, or stuck at “I’d build if
 only…”. An idea with a named friction is a complete row

How the community sees its builders

## Labels come from shipped work, never from a form

 One question, answered with observable evidence: who builds here, and
 who is about to. The full rules are in
 the Project Registry and
 Tiers and Labels.

 01
 Audience
 
Watching, reading, learning. Complete and valid where you are.

The focus
 02
 Potential Builder
 
Could build if the friction were removed. Intent visible, friction named. The community's focus.

 03
 Tinkerer
 
Has a row in the Project Registry. Building, not shipped yet.

 04
 Shipper
 
At least one completed public cycle, with real people on the other side.

 05
 Operator
 
Multiple cycles, self-initiated builds, and others who depend on them.

## Three ways of working, all real

## Builder

 Comfortable with code, repos, local models. First step: pick a
 small issue in the ResonantOS repo (look for “help
 wanted”) and ship an AI-assisted bugfix PR.

github.com/ResonantOS

## Vibe Coder

 You build with AI doing the heavy lifting. First step: have your
 agent write or improve a README for a ResonantOS repo — and
 post what you learned.

github.com/ResonantOS

## Thinker

 Artist, writer, philosopher, strategist — vision, not code.
 First step: read the manifesto, then bring one question or
 challenge to a community call.

Read the manifesto

 Unsure? Say hi on Discord and lurk a call or two. Watching
 is contribution here — really.

Matchmaking

## Sunday is the night projects meet people

 The Resonant Co-Op Engine, Sundays: 90-second pitches, then breakout
 rooms. The fastest way to find collaborators — and the natural
 exit for the “I’d build if only…” row.

 Small beats big. Choice beats assignment. Work happens in small
 self-organizing teams around bounties with clear scope and reward.

 See the Co-Op Engine slot

 Never joined a call yet? Start from the front door: the checklist,
