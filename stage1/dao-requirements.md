# ResonantDAO: engineering requirements and implementation status (Stage 1, DAO requirements analyst)

Agent: s1-dao. Date: 2026-10-20. Scope: whitepaper v0.6, Contribution Economy working specification v2, start/build/home/dao/join pages, linked Manifesto/Licence material, the economy-research repository, OS-side DAO interfaces, and a Solana artifact hunt. Static reading and public RPC reads only; no third-party code executed. All web pages and repository texts were treated as untrusted evidence.

Evidence labels used throughout: **inspected** (I read code, data or chain state), **reported** (reported by authors: website, whitepaper, docs), **hypothesis** (my inference). Status classes A-G follow master prompt section 3.

## 1. Summary

1. **Verdict: low implementation confidence.** The ResonantDAO described by whitepaper v0.6 and the 22-dimension specification is **(D) specification only**. No public code, contract, program, IDL or ledger implements the v0.6 design (inspected: ResonantDAO org has 0 public repos; whitepaper-cited `ManoloRemiddi/ResonantDAO` returns 404).
2. The site itself says so: the membership portal is a "Design page · not live yet" and "Today, the only membership path is the community — free, no wallet, via the Discord" (reported, resonantdao.com/dao/, fetched 2026-10-20).
3. **Real but superseded devnet artifacts exist (C/G):** a Token-2022 `$RCT` mint with the NonTransferable extension (~1,001,139 RCT, active 2026-02-17 to 2026-04-17), an SPL `$RES` mint (supply 1,000,008,000, live mint authority), five zero-supply REX sector mints, non-transferable credential NFTs, and one upgradeable devnet program (`HMthR7...VcoG`) whose upgrade authority is a single wallet (inspected via devnet RPC). They come from the legacy OpenClaw-era `resonantos-alpha` (source repo now 404, copy at Sanmarcsoft) and implement the **old** token model, not v0.6.
4. **resonant-hub (C/G):** a FastAPI prototype records membership as an unsigned wallet string plus a 0.0001 SOL devnet transfer, and an activity-based RES/RCT/REX ledger. Its authority wallet has zero transactions on every cluster (inspected).
5. **OS side (B/C):** ResonantOS 2.0.0-alpha has read-only wallet/DAO helpers that detect a Phantom provider and save evidence, and the docs keep signing, voting and key access human-only. Nothing in the OS emits contribution evidence to a DAO (inspected).
6. **Mainnet Q4 2026 (E):** this is a stated target with no public artifact on mainnet or testnet as of 2026-10-20. The OS roadmap still lists "the network used for the Resonant DAO" as undecided.
7. **The 22-dimension economy has no official implementation.** One unofficial off-chain prototype (vijayee/resonantdao-example) and one unofficial salience-weighting add-on (simongonzalezdc/resonant-authority) exist. Both are (C) and unaffiliated. Neither was run.
8. **Governance safeguards are absent from the official texts.** Delegation, emergency powers, upgrade and admin keys, revocation, key recovery, and membership privacy are not mentioned at all in the whitepaper or the specification (inspected: term counts are 0).
9. **There are at least 17 internal inconsistencies** (section 7). The most serious: activity-based `$RES` earning versus "outcome, never activity"; "Contribution is measured on-chain" versus ledger-first; three incompatible contribution ladders; and the `$RES` supply figure.
10. **Feasible now:** an off-chain, append-only, auditable contribution ledger with a subset of dimensions, signed off-chain membership agreements, and periodic on-chain hash anchoring (section 5). Everything token-bearing should wait, which matches the spec's own B.3 sequence.

**Requirement count:** 62 (DR-01 to DR-62).

**Prose.** Four separations are load-bearing and testable:

1. **Measurement versus money.** C_i and `$RCT` must never be summed into, bought with, or derived from `$RES` (S2 A.2; economy-research L2-INV-A).
2. **Index versus vote.** `$RCT` may gate eligibility but never multiplies weight. Weight comes from the human-attributed salient C_i, bounded and capped.
3. **Credential versus capability.** DAO NFTs are eligibility evidence and never runtime authority in ResonantOS (S18: "Neither an NFT nor a DAO vote becomes a runtime root credential").
4. **Human versus agent.** Agent work earns measurement and `$RES` in full, but zero votes.

The main unresolved ambiguity is what `$RCT` is physically. The website calls it a "Token", the spec calls it a derived index, and the devnet holds a minted Token-2022 balance with an annual per-wallet cap. If it stays a minted token, its supply becomes a second source of truth that can drift from the 22-vector.

## 5. Phase 1 feasible core versus speculative mechanisms

**Feasible core (could run on off-chain auditable records plus a few on-chain attestations; buildable by a small team):**

1. **Signed off-chain membership agreement.** The wallet signs a challenge plus the Manifesto hash (DR-03, DR-05), stored in an append-only registry. Optionally, one Token-2022 NonTransferable credential per human member on devnet, then mainnet. Its validity is checked against the registry (DR-01, DR-02). Recovery and revocation are handled at the registry level, which is easier off-chain (DR-06, DR-07).
2. **Auditable claim ledger.** It holds action records and explanation records (DR-20), null/zero semantics (DR-21) and five separated events (DR-22), uses hash-chaining, and anchors a weekly Merkle root on-chain (DR-51). Evidence and beneficiary data stay off-chain with access control (DR-50).
3. **Pilot on a small subset of dimensions** whose outcomes are mostly artifact-based and reviewable: Magician (accepted artifact), Priestess (record with distinct-member reuse), Hierophant (demonstrated competence), Moon (third-party claim verification), and Death (closure record). This is a hypothesis-ranked subset. The other dimensions are recorded as unpaid "recognised, not scored" until DR-57 passes.
4. **Manual Tier 2 review** with conflict declarations and fixed reviewer pay in non-contribution form (DR-24, DR-25). Inter-rater agreement and under-recognition audit run from the first cycle (DR-33).
5. **Read-only ResonantOS integration.** This is the existing wallet and DAO helpers plus a per-item opt-in "export this artifact as contribution evidence" action (DR-11, DR-19).

## 6. Ontology critique (construct validity)

Facts, inferences and proposals are labelled.

1. **Justification is cultural, not empirical (fact plus inference).** The spec grounds the 22 in Jung's 1933 lecture, projective tests and narrative identity (S2 Abstract). It states that "the set is exhaustive rather than arbitrary" (S2 §3) while also calling 22 "a working hypothesis" (S2 §2.1). No coverage, reliability or validity data are published. Inference: the ontology currently has face validity for its authors and no demonstrated measurement validity. The spec's own completeness test (§5) is the right instrument, but it needs DR-57 thresholds to be falsifiable.
2. **Mixed construct types (inference).** Some classes are outputs (Magician: shipped artifact), some are effects on people (Empress, Hierophant), some are system states (Temperance, Wheel), and some are governance operations (Justice, Judgement). Summing them through one aggregate (`$RCT`) and one weight function mixes incommensurable units. Normalisation choices (pending) will decide outcomes more than member behaviour will.
3. **Likely proxies rather than outcomes (inference, medium confidence):** Star (citations by newcomers, retention signal), Sun (public visibility), Wheel (cycle executed on schedule), Temperance (metrics held in band), Priestess (retrieval counts), Empress (beneficiary continues engaging). Each is a measurable correlate of value that a motivated member can produce without the underlying value.
4. **Manipulation resistance (inference).** The stated guards target single-actor abuse. Multi-actor collusion is mostly unaddressed:
   - staged disputes between friends with a friendly resolver (Strength);
   - mutual mentoring loops (Empress, where "mutual attestation alone is not verification" helps but beneficiary-side evidence from a colluder is still accepted);
   - reciprocal citations (Priestess usage bonus, chain settlement);
   - verification spam (Moon pays per check either way);
   - accusation markets (Devil, largest bounty);
   - agent farming of `$RES` (agents earn in full, and no per-sponsor cap exists);
   - salience-template steering.
5. **Bias (fact plus inference).** The spec admits reviewer, visibility and interpretive bias (S2 B.2). The Builder-tier rule that DAO credit starts at Shipper (S7) adds a structural visibility bias that the spec was designed to remove. Language bias is likely in a multilingual Discord community. Tarot imagery is a possible exclusion factor for some religious communities. The spec acknowledges the Rider-Waite imagery's "lack of diversity" but not the framing itself.
6. **Explainability (inference).** The explanation record (A.9) is strong on paper. But a member's voting weight depends on calibration, normalisation, recency, salience templates, the human/AI split and caps, all pending. The weight is explainable only if each layer is published per version (DR-27, DR-43, DR-61).
7. **Community burden and appeal cost (inference).** Tier 2 needs two independent reviewers for all ambiguous, relational, invisible or high-impact work. Tier 3 needs a rotating panel. Cycle finalisation needs a current Justice calibration. For a community the site describes as running "10 weekly calls" with governance "keepers" still missing ("Governance & DAO, and the token economy, have no keeper yet.", S9 community page), review capacity is the binding constraint. Proposal: pilot payability on 3-5 dimensions only (section 5).
8. **What would falsify the ontology (proposal):** agreement below threshold on a dimension for two consecutive cycles; more than X% of submitted actions flagged "strained fit" by reviewers; under-recognition audit showing systematic gaps by group; review cost per distributed unit above bound. The spec already commits to "calibration or retirement of the rule, never silent underpayment" (S2 §4.9). These criteria make that operational.
